Hollow Way: a house stepped down the slope, so no room sits below ground.

Location
Hebden Bridge, West Yorkshire
Type
New build
Size
158 m²
Year
2022
Budget
£560,000 to £620,000
Role
Design and delivery

The brief

Kit and Morgan Ashworth came to us with a steep hillside plot above Hebden Bridge that every other architect had filled with a house dug into the hill, with its back rooms underground.

The challenge

The plot falls one metre in every three, down to a narrow lane. Digging in would have meant dark rooms at the back and huge retaining walls. The only access for a lorry was a single-track road with a weight limit.

What we did

  1. Design

    The house steps down the hill in three levels, each one a little further forward, so every room has a window to the valley and none sits below ground. The roof of each level is the garden of the one above.

  2. Materials

    Dark gritstone reclaimed from the old walls on the site, charred timber and sedum roofs. Reusing the stone saved more than two hundred lorry trips up the lane.

  3. Build

    Calder Valley Builders built it in fifteen months, through a wet Pennine winter. We stepped the foundations so each level could be built and roofed before the next began.

The result

28 months

2.4% over budget
