# BRIEF: Ostra hero

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** The user said everything needed is in /docs. Every decision below comes from the brief, copy deck, design system and sitemap. Nothing is quoted from an interview.

1. **Vibe:** quiet, tactile, deliberate, exact. References: the user's three competitor screenshots (Heron AI's ruled grid, mono labels, drawing-first imagery, one accent).
2. **Journey (hero only):** recognition (a house drawing itself) → turn (the day moves and light lands in the court) → resolution (the philosophy section: "A house should fit the way you live.").
3. **Energy:** calm throughout. The only rising action is the sun crossing the sheet.
4. **Feeling curve:**
   - Load: *curiosity*. Footprints draw, walls rise.
   - Early scroll: *settling*. Material hatching and trees fill in.
   - Mid scroll: *recognition*. "Light lands where you sit." The shadows swing across the court.
   - Release: *trust*. The philosophy copy explains why that matters.
   - **Peak:** "You scroll and the sun moves across the house." This is the longest span of the pin.
5. **Signature move:** scroll is the time of day. Shadows and wall shading are computed live from the same 3D geometry the ink was rendered from.
6. **Range:** editorial, close to the competitors' drawing-sheet register, held to design system 1.0.
7. **Structure:** one pinned scene, not a continuous world.
8. **Assets:** none supplied, and no image-generation key. The world is drawn, not photographed, which the design system prefers ("measured drawings, no stock imagery").

**Tell-someone sentence:** "It's the site where you scroll and the sun moves across the house."
