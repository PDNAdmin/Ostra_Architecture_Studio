Ashdown Studio: a garden studio lit from the north, so the light stays even all day.

Location
Forest Row, East Sussex
Type
New build
Size
32 m²
Year
2022
Budget
£58,000 to £65,000
Role
Design and delivery

The brief

Lena Vos came to us with a birch wood at the end of a garden in Forest Row that was the only quiet place she had to paint, but the light in her old shed changed every hour.

The challenge

Lena paints for galleries and needs light that stays the same all day. The trees she loves around the studio cast moving shade, and it had to stay small enough to need no planning permission.

What we did

  1. Design

    All the light comes from the north, through a long run of roof lights along the ridge. North light barely changes through the day, so colours read the same at nine as at four. One low window looks into the birches at eye level when she sits down.

  2. Materials

    Larch outside, birch plywood inside, both left bare. The floor is cork, warm underfoot and quiet. The studio sits on screw piles, so no tree roots were cut.

  3. Build

    Wealden Timber Frame built it in ten weeks, mostly in their workshop. It went up on site in four days.
