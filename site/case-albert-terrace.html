14 Albert Terrace: a glass room that turns a terrace house toward its garden.

Location
Whitstable, Kent
Type
Extension
Size
38 m² added
Year
2023
Budget
£140,000 to £160,000
Role
Design and delivery

The brief

Grace Moreno came to us with a Victorian terrace house in Whitstable that had a kitchen at the back that never saw the sun, and a garden she only used in August.

The challenge

The house shares both side walls, so every change needed party wall agreements with two neighbours. There is no side access: every steel beam and pane of glass had to come through the house.

What we did

  1. Design

    A single glass room across the back of the house turns it toward the garden. The kitchen moved into it, and the old kitchen became a quiet snug. Glass is set straight into the brick, so the room feels like part of the garden.

  2. Materials

    Frameless triple glazing, a slim steel roof and a polished concrete floor with heating underneath. We used reclaimed London stock bricks to rebuild the garden walls, so the new room sits against old, warm brick.

  3. Build

    Harbour Builders built it in sixteen weeks. Every piece of glass was sized to fit through the front door, and we agreed the party wall details with both neighbours before work began.

The result
