Mill Lane: a low garden wing, so the old house could stay as it was.

Location
Stroud, Gloucestershire
Type
Remodel
Size
164 m²
Year
2024
Budget
£310,000 to £350,000
Role
Design and delivery

The brief

Tom and Ellie Hart came to us with a Cotswold stone cottage in Stroud that had a dark kitchen at the back and no way to reach the garden without going outside.

The challenge

The cottage is in a conservation area, and the Harts loved it as it was. They needed more room and light without changing the face of the house, and they had to keep living there with two young children while the work was done.

What we did

  1. Design

    We left the cottage almost untouched and added a low garden wing beside it for the kitchen and living room. A short glass link joins old and new, so you step from the cottage straight into the light and out onto the lawn.

  2. Materials

    Vertical larch cladding and a Cotswold stone plinth let the wing sit quietly next to the cottage. Inside, an oak kitchen and a concrete floor that holds the sun’s warmth. We insulated the cottage at the same time, from the inside, with breathable wood fibre.

  3. Build

    Coombe & Sons built the wing in eight months while the family lived next door. Marcus called the Harts every Monday with the week ahead, and the only disruption inside the cottage lasted four days.

The result

First sketch to handover
14 months
Final cost against budget
3.1% over budget, for two roof lights the Harts added during the build
Energy or comfort
Heating bills down 46% on the year before, for a house a third larger
