What we use now

Your cookie choice (ostra-cookies, stored in your browser). Remembers whether you chose Accept all or Essential only, so we do not ask again. Essential.

Analytics. None at the moment. If we add them, they will only load after you choose Accept all, and each cookie will be listed here first.

Changing your choice

Use the Manage link in the cookie banner, or clear this site's data in your browser settings and choose again.

Third parties

Fonts load from Google Fonts. They do not set cookies, but Google receives your IP address. See our privacy page.
