Homes that costless to live in.

Good design cuts energy use before any technology is added: orientation, insulation, daylight and materials that last.

Four things we design in from the first sketch

  1. 01

    Orientation

    We place rooms by the sun, so the house warms itself in winter and stays cool in summer.

  2. 02

    Insulation and airtightness

    A well-sealed, well-insulated shell cuts the heat a house needs before any equipment is chosen.

  3. 03

    Daylight

    Rooms shaped for daylight need less electric light, and they feel better to be in.

  4. 04

    Materials that last

    Materials that age well and can be repaired don't need replacing, which is the lowest-carbon choice of all.

Why we insulate before adding solar panels
