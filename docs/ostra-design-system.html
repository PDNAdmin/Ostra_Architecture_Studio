Where the brief and the directive disagree

Accent colour

Ostra's brief asks for no accent competing with photography. The directive asks for a high-voltage accent. Resolved: safety orange is limited to conversion triggers, laser borders, active telemetry and data points, with cyan for tertiary and focus states. Neither sits on or over an image, and gallery views drop controls to ink.

Warm paper, black ink

The brief wants warm materials, not cold white. The canvas is bone, a warm off-white, and text is a brown-biased near-black ink, not pure black. Orange is a fill: on bone it is 2.7:1, so orange text uses Signal ink. Dark plates are kept only inside drawings that need them, such as the orange render layer and the loader.

Slow versus kinetic

The brief wants slow, purposeful motion. Magnetic buttons, cursor followers and border beams stay on UI controls, turn off for touch and reduced motion, and never touch photographs. Image reveals use the ink curve at 880 ms.

Conversion focus

Ostra is an editorial portfolio. The conversion event is the consultation form, so the system spends its clarity on that form and the flagship case studies.

Ink-bleed mask reveal

Process · Drawing first

Measured before it is built.

Every project starts as a drawing the client can read.

An organic blob mask grows from the centre of the panel to cover all four corners, driven by scroll position through the ink curve (cubic-bezier 0.19, 1, 0.22, 1). Replay runs it once over 1.4 s. With reduced motion it sits fully revealed.

Blueprint to render lens

Hover or drag over the drawing

Both layers share one projection, so the render lands exactly on the drawing. The lens is the same organic blob mask as the ink-bleed reveal, following the pointer and growing on the ink curve. The render adds rammed earth, oak and lime plaster, glazing, planting, paving and cast shadows. Touch: drag. Keyboard: focus the drawing and use the arrow keys. Materials and plot are sample data.

Ink sketch to orange render

Hover or drag over the sketch

Two drawings of one building, built from the same geometry. The sketch is loose pen work on paper: doubled, wandering lines and hatched shade. Under the lens the same lines resolve into single clean orange strokes, the black hatching becomes orange shade that deepens under the roof and floor slabs, and the glass picks up a warm gradient. The lens is the ink-bleed blob mask. A first-view sweep hints at the interaction and is skipped with reduced motion. Touch: drag. Keyboard: focus the drawing and use the arrow keys. Building is sample artwork.
